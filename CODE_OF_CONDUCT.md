# Code of Conduct

AgentPause is a research project about human agency. We want the community around it
to reflect the same values: respect, transparency, and care for the people affected by
our work.

This code of conduct is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
(licensed CC BY 4.0).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints, methods, and research traditions.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback, including critique of research claims.
- Credit prior work accurately. Do not overstate novelty or results.
- Protect study participants: never share participant data, even pseudonymous data,
  in issues, pull requests, or discussions.
- Focus on what is best for the community and for the people who use agents built with
  this toolkit.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or private.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Fabricating, misrepresenting, or selectively omitting results.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may
remove, edit, or reject contributions that do not align with this code of conduct.

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers through the contact listed in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) (use the private channel and mark the report "Conduct").
All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.

Maintainers will follow the Contributor Covenant enforcement ladder: correction, warning,
temporary ban, permanent ban, depending on severity and history.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies within all project spaces and when an individual officially
represents the project in public spaces.
